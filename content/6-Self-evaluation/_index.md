During my internship in the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program from 14/09/2026 to 10/12/2026, I had the opportunity to apply my university knowledge in a real-world working environment. I started with foundational labs on IAM, EC2, S3, and VPC, then moved on to collaborating with my team to build a serverless chatbot supporting legal and data security lookup.

Through this process, I gained a clearer understanding of how AWS services work together and how to balance cost, security, and operability when choosing an architecture. I also built habits around testing, monitoring logs and costs, and keeping weekly worklogs consistently.

In terms of work ethic, I strove to complete tasks on time, follow program guidelines, proactively incorporate feedback from mentors, and coordinate with teammates to improve the product.

### Needs Improvement

- Dive deeper into security and networking on AWS (advanced IAM, VPC, endpoints), while getting familiar with Infrastructure as Code such as CloudFormation or CDK for fast, repeatable deployments instead of relying solely on console operations.
- Be more proactive in proposing ideas and reaching out to mentors early when facing blockers, rather than struggling alone for too long before discussing.
- Manage time and documentation more effectively: write notes, worklogs, and technical documentation consistently throughout the week to avoid piling everything up at the weekend.
